Pixel Shift
===========

Shoots a series of 2 or 4 frames with sub-pixel lens motor displacement
between shots, enabling super-resolution merge in post-processing.

Works on Canon 50D and other EF-mount cameras with Magic Lantern.

Usage
-----

Enable the module, then go to Shoot > Pixel Shift > Shoot Now.
